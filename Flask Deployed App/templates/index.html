📸 Tips for Accurate Diagnosis
1
Focus on the symptoms: Ensure the discolored, spotted, or infected portion of the leaf is clearly visible in the center.
2
Natural lighting: Avoid harsh flash glare or dark shadows. Diffused natural daylight works best.
3
Clean background: A plain background (like your hand or tabletop) helps the neural network focus on the leaf margins.
