System Architecture & Model Topology

The convolutional network processes normalized 224×224×3 RGB tensor representations. The backbone comprises four sequential blocks, each containing two 3×3 convolutions, rectified linear activations (ReLU), Batch Normalization for covariate shift mitigation, and 2×2 Max Pooling.

The spatial feature maps flatten to 50,176 latent dimensions before passing into a dense fully-connected projection with 40% dropout regularization, concluding in a 39-way softmax distribution over 38 foliar pathology classes plus a background non-leaf rejection class.

Input: Tensor(1, 3, 224, 224)
↓ ConvBlock 1: (32 channels, ReLU, BN, MaxPool)
↓ ConvBlock 2: (64 channels, ReLU, BN, MaxPool)
↓ ConvBlock 3: (128 channels, ReLU, BN, MaxPool)
↓ ConvBlock 4: (256 channels, ReLU, BN, MaxPool)
↓ Flatten: 50,176
↓ Linear: 1024 (Dropout 0.4)
↓ Output: 39 Classes (Softmax)
